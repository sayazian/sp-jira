# Standard Pioneer — Founders' operating context

**Context date:** 2026-10-12  
**Workspace:** ChatGPT Project "SP Tickets" and GitHub repository `sayazian/sp-jira`.

## Why this exists
Standard Pioneer is an early-stage initiative exploring how to help organizations adopt AI-native workflows. The founders are developing shared technical capability while exploring business direction. They want to move forward visibly without losing experimentation, creativity or individual autonomy.

This repository is **not** a bootcamp for external participants. The October–November roadmap is a practical plan for founders to get started with Cloudflare OS, agents, integrations, collaboration and security.

## Working agreement (proposed process)
- **Shared outcomes, individual autonomy:** The group agrees on what is useful and demonstrable. Owners decide how to implement.
- **Visibility without surveillance:** Use issues to record commitments, results and blockers; avoid time tracking or monitoring personal activity.
- **Evidence-based completion:** A working demo, reproducible test or documented investigation should substantiate the outcome.
- **Early transparency:** Raise likely delays or dependencies before a target session; replan together when necessary.
- **Exploration is valid:** An unsuccessful technical experiment can be valuable when the findings and recommendation are explicit.
- **Decisions with impact on shared priorities, dates, architecture or resources** should be brought to the founders; local implementation choices stay with owners.

## Suggested session rhythm
1. **Before the session:** Owners update issue status, evidence and blockers.
2. **Opening (~10 min):** Review last session's commitments and deviations.
3. **Main discussion (~30–40 min):** Show working outputs, discoveries and technical decisions.
4. **Closing (~10 min):** Agree on the next outputs, owners and target sessions.
5. **After the session:** Coordinator posts a concise summary and updates action items.

This is a suggested operating rhythm, not a fixed meeting calendar. Adapt it to actual founder availability.

## Roles and boundaries
The session coordinator facilitates visibility and records actions; **each founder remains accountable for their own commitments**. Assign owners by agreement. Do not treat the coordinator as a manager or sole guarantor of delivery.

## Technical workstreams

See `docs/milestones.md` for dates and issue references. The 10 introductory agents are distinct from the original curriculum's separate 11 practical-agent exercises, which are outside this initial plan.
